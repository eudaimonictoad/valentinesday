Twenty Ensigns of the Nations · Uncaptioned
Pocket Library Series · No. 11 · The Reader is Trusted to Know Them

The engraver was paid by the flag and not by the name, and so no names are given. Each figure turns over. On the back of every one the engraver has left a remark in the pen-and-dot hand of the Freemasons; the key to that hand is published separately, and the reader who has not yet got it may look and wonder.
