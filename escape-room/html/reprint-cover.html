How to use this file. The pages after this one are in packet order. Print the lot, then swap each set for the pages it replaces. Everything not listed here is unchanged, so keep what you already printed.

What changed

Packet pagesSheetsWhat and why
3-42Cipher plates I and II
Rim text no longer runs into the cut line; the rim and Plate II now say which ring is written and which is meant; the hour hand points straight at the 3.
5-1511Parlour songbook
The Barista's Hornpipe moves from air 1 to air 12, so it is no longer the first thing she sees. Its two note numbers are still 12 and 15.
16-172Stars and their notes
Ed Sheeran moves from near the top of the list to 26th of 32.
26-272Cartomancer's guide
The Jokers are set as a fifth suit instead of a box, and the real one is The Coloured Joker.
291Pig-pen alphabet
The dots no longer collide with the letters or the gridlines.
The songbook is optional. Your printed copy still works: the Barista's Hornpipe still numbers the dotted half note 12 and the pair of beamed eighths 15, which is all the puzzle needs. Reprint those eleven pages only if you want that air buried in the middle rather than sitting first. If you skip it, also skip the Stars sheet, since the two were regenerated together.
Not in this file: the game-master pages at the back of the packet (the wall to trace, the setup guide, the cipher solution). Reprint those from the full packet whenever you like; they are for you, not for her.
