The four keys

  • BLACK opens the suitcase. Hidden in the toilet cistern, reached by the Queen of Hearts.
  • DARK BLUE opens the backpack. Hidden in the Dutch oven beside Plate I.
  • PURPLE opens the compartment inside the backpack. Taped behind the Milk Crate painting, the very first thing she was handed. The cipher wheel is what tells her.
  • LIGHT BLUE is not used. Keep it in your pocket.

Three branches, then the lock

  • Branch A, music, ends at the backgammon set with Plate II.
  • Branch B, names, ends with the pen, the pigpen sheet and the black key.
  • Branch C, the knife, ends at the Dutch oven with Plate I and the dark blue key.
  • The wheel needs Plate I, Plate II and the time. The time comes from the flags. So all three branches must finish.

The final card, in wheel symbols

MFF6 DW5OY7 H5W LO8QH 9KOYHOYA

Reads: LOOK BEHIND THE FIRST PAINTING. Copy those symbols by hand onto a card and hide it under the meditation cushion.

Before she arrives

  • Weigh the knife. It must read 79 g. The nearest entries are 58 g and 99 g.
  • Check pages 12 and 15 of your copy of The Prophet against the concordance.
  • Test the blue-light pen on the wall, and that it wipes off.
  • Cut one spare lemon to be sure the joker survives.
  • Assemble the wheel at 3:30 and decode the card yourself.
