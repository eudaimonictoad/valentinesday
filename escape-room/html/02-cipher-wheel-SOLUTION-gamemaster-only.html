How it works. Plate II (the hands) is pinned on top of Plate I (the dial) and turned until the hands read 3:30 on the dial's clock numerals: the short hand pointing straight at the 3 and the long hand straight at the 6. That is a turn of 200° clockwise from the printed position. The rim of the dial says find it without, read it within: a secret message is written in dial symbols; for each one, find it on the outer dial and read the symbol lined up with it on the inner disc. Letters and digits both take part, so you can hide a number, a time, or a word.

To make your own: python3 escape-room/src/cipher_data.py YOUR MESSAGE HERE
Plate II's hands are deliberately printed at an angle that reads as no sensible time; only when turned 200° do they show 3:30. If you would rather a different answer, change SOLUTION_TIME, HOUR_ANGLE, MINUTE_ANGLE and ROT in src/cipher_data.py (ROT must be a multiple of 10) and rebuild.
