Why this changed. Both flag paintings were in the candy jar, so the Chloé Zhao step was carrying three things at once: the blue-light pen and both paintings. Miss it and the endgame is dead. The jar now holds the pen and a framer's delivery card, and the card says where the two paintings are. Two corrections in the same guide

The tear-off count was wrong. The old guide told you to tear the last six leaves out of the packet. The game-master section is seven — the wall tracing sheet, five guide pages and the cipher solution. Following the old count leaves the wall sheet in her packet, and that sheet spells out BREATHE / BREATHE / GREEN / SIT. Count seven from the back.
Two flowchart boxes were printing on top of each other. The line beginning “ALIEN BLUES in marker…” was half again wider than its box and ran across the box beside it and off the page. Both are rewritten to fit, and the builder now refuses to draw a line too wide for its box.
